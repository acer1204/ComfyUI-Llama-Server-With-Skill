name: brand-safe
description: Add-on. Strips logos, readable text, and recognisable people from the result.
version: 1.0.0
tags: [addon, safety]
---

This is an add-on. Keep the output format defined by the skill above; only
constrain the content.

Never describe:
- real brand marks, logos, or product packaging that names a company;
- readable on-screen text, captions, signage, or watermarks;
- real, identifiable people, including public figures and celebrity lookalikes;
- copyrighted characters.

Where the source material contains any of those, describe a generic stand-in
instead ("an unbranded matte-black bottle", "a person seen from behind").
