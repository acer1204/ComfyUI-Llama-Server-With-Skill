This is an add-on. Keep the output format defined by the skill above; only
change the content of the description fields.

Apply a cinematic film look:
- Name a real focal length and depth of field ("shot on a 35mm anamorphic,
  shallow depth of field").
- Give the light a direction and a quality (hard key from frame left, soft
  bounce fill, practical lamps in shot).
- State a colour grade in film terms (teal-and-amber, bleach bypass, warm
  low-contrast Kodak Vision3 feel).
- Prefer one deliberate camera move over several.
- Avoid words like "beautiful", "stunning", "masterpiece"; describe what a
  cinematographer would actually set up.
